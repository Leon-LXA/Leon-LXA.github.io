**ETH Zurich · April – June 2023**<br>
**Advisor:** Dr. Vinzenz Thoma, D-INFK and ETH AI Center

I reimplemented the Adaptive Mechanism Design (AMD) algorithm using Ray RLlib, with support for multi-agent environments. The implementation was validated on the prisoner's dilemma and extended to more complex environments.

I also provided open-source implementations of the Wolfpack and Gathering environments for studying sequential social dilemmas.
