**Captain, Team WTR · Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen · December 2019 – March 2021**<br>
**Advisor:** Prof. Yunjiang Lou

I led Team WTR in the ABU ROBOCON 2020 competition, developing two robots for the rugby-themed challenge. The team won first prize and placed among the top 16 of 75 participating universities.

I previously participated in ROBOCON 2019, when the team won a national second prize. Beyond the competition, I established a campus robotics forum that attracted more than 300 students.
