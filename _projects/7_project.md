**Master's Thesis · ETH Zurich · August 2024 – February 2025**<br>
**Advisor:** Prof. Mirko Meboldt, Product Development Group Zurich (pd|z)<br>
**Grade:** 6.0/6.0

My master's thesis explored synthetic data generation for recognizing assembly actions. I used the GraspXL framework for reinforcement-learning-based dexterous grasp generation, synthesized assembly motions with a grasping hand in simulation, and trained action recognition models with the resulting motions and labels.

### Industrial validation

During my subsequent R&D engineering internship at Inspire AG, from February to September 2025, I developed an assembly motion recognition and real-time error detection system with Bossard AG and Intelliact AG. The system was validated through field tests at Stäubli and Mettler-Toledo factories under the supervision of Dr. Julian Ferchow.

The related paper, *A Synthetic-Driven Vision System for Assembly Step Recognition*, was published at CASE 2026. Hui Zhang and I contributed equally.
