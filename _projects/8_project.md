**Semester Project · ETH Zurich · November 2023 – March 2024**<br>
**Advisor:** Prof. Fan Shi, ETH AI Center

I studied adversarial attacks on quadruped locomotion policies using gradient information from differentiable simulation. The project applied projected gradient descent to joint-state inputs to find perturbations that disrupt the robot's locomotion and induce falls.

This work investigated policy robustness in a setting where the simulator provides accurate gradient information for constructing adversarial perturbations.
