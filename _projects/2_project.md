**Research Assistant · Tsinghua University · June 2021 – September 2022**<br>
**Advisor:** Prof. Xueqian Wang

I worked on real-time SLAM and navigation for an AgileX SCOUT mobile robot operating on rough terrain. The system combined point-cloud mapping with local and global motion planning.

- Developed local collision-avoidance planning using model predictive control (MPC) and control barrier functions (CBFs).
- Used PF-RRT* for global planning and point-cloud representations for mapping complex terrain.
- Worked on integration and real-world navigation on the mobile robot.
